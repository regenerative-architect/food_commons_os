Build food resilience without trading one dependency for another

A city that imports nearly everything is vulnerable when a road closes. A city that grows everything itself is vulnerable to a single bad harvest. Real resilience comes from distributed food sovereignty: local capacity, regional exchange, and international trade that reinforce each other instead of replacing one single point of failure with another.

This dashboard turns that framework into something a government, cooperative, or institution can actually use: a shared map of who does what, a self-assessment, a governance checklist, a 90-day pilot tracker, and evaluation indicators — all editable, all saved on your own device, none of it sent anywhere.

Zero-Harm & Anti-Inversion note

This tool is for building voluntary, reciprocal food resilience — not for gatekeeping food, compelling labor, or concentrating control over land, harvests, or distribution. Any adaptation of this file that could be used to coerce, surveil, or exclude should be treated as a misuse of the design, not a feature of it. Real authority over land, safety, and food distribution stays with communities, producers, and the applicable regulators — this dashboard only helps coordinate and remember decisions.

Privacy & data

All entries save locally (IndexedDB, with a localStorage fallback) on this device only. Nothing is transmitted, no analytics run by default, and no account is required. Use "Reset local data" to erase everything this page has stored, at any time.
